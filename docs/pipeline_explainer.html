The Lidl cut needed all three. Layer 1 flagged two clips off slot, layer 2 showed the cut sitting 371 ms past the return of sound, and only the eye settled it: the advert began 17 frames earlier, and two independently written detectors had made the same mistake.

Where it stopped working, and why

median difference from stated length, by year · September 2026 run

In the September run, clips from 2025 and 2026 were further from their stated length than clips from earlier years. This was first read as a change in the adverts. The cause was in the files. In most recent recordings the video stream starts later than the audio, and the picture detector counts from the first video frame while every other step keeps time from the start of the file, so it had been looking about a second late.

Fixed in the October full run. Allowing for the late video start, and searching the whole silence for the first frame, brought every year to between 81.6% and 84.7% of clips within 0.2 s; on 19 cut points checked by hand, 16 now fall exactly on the first frame and the other three are one frame away.
